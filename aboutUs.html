WHO WE ARE

More Than a Sport. A Journey of Excellence.

Olive Shooting Academy is a premium shooting academy promoted by former military officers with a vision to develop disciplined, confident, and high-performing athletes through the sport of shooting.

We believe shooting is not just a sport. It is a journey that develops focus, patience, emotional control, mental strength, discipline, and character.

As an internationally recognized Olympic sport, shooting provides young individuals with an opportunity to develop valuable life skills while pursuing excellence at national and international levels.

At Olive Shooting Academy, we provide a safe, disciplined, and motivating environment where aspiring shooters can discover their potential, develop their skills, and work towards their sporting ambitions under the guidance of qualified and experienced coaches.

Col. KK Singh (Retd.)

Director

DIRECTOR'S MESSAGE

Aim with Precision, Rise with Confidence!

Dear Shooters,

Welcome to Olive Shooting Academy.

We recognize that shooting is not just a game, but a journey that develops discipline, mental strength, focus, and character. As an internationally recognized Olympic sport, shooting helps young individuals develop concentration, emotional control, patience, and decision-making abilities.

The sport of shooting has consistently brought international recognition to India through the Olympic Games, Asian Games, Commonwealth Games, and National Championships.

With growing opportunities for shooters, including scholarships, career pathways, and opportunities in government and private-sector employment, shooting offers a promising future for young athletes who aspire to excel and bring pride to the country at national and international levels.

Our state-of-the-art academy provides professional training in a safe, disciplined, and motivating environment under the guidance of qualified coaches.

Here is a wonderful opportunity to begin your journey towards a successful career in shooting.

Best Wishes and Happy Shooting!
